## Audit corrections covered

- H-1: short final cues no longer overlap; the previous cue is trimmed/merged or the short cue is dropped explicitly. Word timestamps are integer, monotonic and inside the cue.
- H-2: caption load/export/page/worker filter the current narration take; approval/generation require approved READY voice; DB enforces JSON/column take match and same-episode take; voice switching rebinds with a new version.
- M-3: matched/unmatched word timing is clamped to episode duration and cue bounds.
- M-4: unmatched tails are compressed and truncated at `last ASR match + 1.5 s`; low coverage downgrades to `ASR_FALLBACK`.
- M-1/M-2: CJK, emoji and long narrow tokens wrap within 880 px and 42 characters; real frame test covers CJK/emoji.
- M-5: CTA window is capped at 7 s per cue; an insufficient tight window fails explicitly.
- M-6: DB trigger and composite FK ensure the narration take belongs to the caption episode.
- H5: E2E manifest assertions, CTA window tests, real frame safe-area measurement; docs distinguish asserted vs observed cue counts.
