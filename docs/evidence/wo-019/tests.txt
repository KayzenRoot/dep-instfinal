## Database / retention

- PgTAP: owner read, direct write denied, second owner isolated, revision conflict, APPROVED

- The RPC receives the exact choices snapshot and rejects concurrent snapshot drift.
- Quota/prune are service-only; the UI lists the latest 30 revisions but approved is fetched
  independently, so it does not disappear behind newer drafts.

## Rendering / limits measured locally

- ASS uses 1080×1920 PlayRes and the TikTok safe area `{x:48,y:180,width:984,height:1500}`.
- The card window must end exactly at `max(clip.timelineEndMs)` and equal the configured duration;
  narration is muted by default only during that window, with or without SFX events.
- SFX is a deterministic 48 kHz stereo 16-bit WAV synthesized in-process; the mix applies
  `loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5` and `alimiter=0.891`.
- `restore`/`approve` carry the source choice revision and cannot rebase a stale card.
- No generated card bytes are stored; only config, choice snapshot and hash.

## Security / privacy

- `choice_end_cards` RLS owner-read/service-write; direct authenticated insert/update denied.
- `/api/end-cards/[revisionId]` owner-only, `private, no-store`, no permanent URL; anonymous denied
  in E2E; stale choices return 409.
- ASS text is stripped of control characters and braces/backslashes are escaped.
- No vote scraping, no social posting, no paid API/GPU.

## Limits verified

- Labels come from V1 choices; long labels that do not fit are rejected with a layout suggestion.
- The card is positioned at the end of the episode; no drag positioning in this WO.
- E2E covers the attach action, not a full MP4 render with video assets attached.
- No golden image across hosts/fonts; UI preview is an approximation of the ASS.
