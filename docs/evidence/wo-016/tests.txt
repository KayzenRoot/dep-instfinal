## QA and cost

- `COLOR_RANGE` is emitted only for plans with `color`; sampled RGB stats are stored
  as evidence and clipping ≥20% at either extreme, or ≥30% combined, becomes WARN
  (never BLOCKED). The sampling is 12 frames at 0.2 fps and is coarse by design.
- `docs/evidence/wo-016/benchmark-color.json` (Windows 11, Ryzen 5 2500U, FFmpeg 9.0.2):
  - 720p plain: 63.1 s per footage minute, 18.2 CPU s, 158 MiB RSS.
  - 720p `cursed_tape@80`: 160.4 s per footage minute, 61.8 CPU s, 172 MiB RSS.
  - 1080p plain: 111.0 s per footage minute, 34.7 CPU s, 275 MiB RSS.
  - 1080p `cursed_tape@80`: 375.2 s per footage minute, 133.2 CPU s, 301 MiB RSS.
  - ARM64 CI (`color-render` run `44508652844`): 8.12→40.82 s/min at 720p and
    16.32→83.66 s/min at 1080p; CPU 3.51/17.62 s and 7.26/38.71 s. Linux FFmpeg did
    not emit `maxrss`, so RSS is null in the CI artifact.
  - The ARM64 workflow `color-render-benchmark` uses `pipefail` and repeats the
    measurement in CI.

## Limits verified

- Color Match is preset + strength + per-scene override, not automatic histogram
  matching; the reference anchors the before/after comparison.
- The side-by-side preview uses approximate CSS; the FFmpeg render is the reference
  and also applies the episode's global VHS layer, which the preview does not show.
- HDR/BT.2020 is out of scope; the pipeline operates on SDR `yuv420p`.
- Clipping is only signalled by post-render QA; there is no pre-render clipping
  analysis, automatic lighter-preset fallback, or critical gating on `COLOR_RANGE`.
- Saving or clearing color creates a DRAFT version; the owner must approve the plan
  and reapprove captions before rendering.
- Preset parameters are v1; changing them requires a version bump and dispatch.
